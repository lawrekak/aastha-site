## 6. Articles on legal-knowledge websites (the right way to earn links)

These links come from writing, not from placing links. They are also the only link-building that fits Rule 36, because an advocate may publish articles.
- **Where:** law blogs and portals that accept guest articles from advocates, such as SCC Online Blog guest columns, Bar and Bench or LiveLaw opinion pieces, Lawctopus, iPleaders, and Uttarakhand newspapers' legal columns (Amar Ujala, Dainik Jagran, Hindustan Times Dehradun).
- **Topic:** a longer version of one of her blog posts, such as a recent Uttarakhand High Court ruling explained, or a UCC or land-law change.
- **Author line**, if the publication allows one:
  > Aastha Vishwakarma is an advocate practising before the High Court of Uttarakhand at Nainital and the Delhi High Court. advaasthavishwakarma.in
- Never pay for placement. Never ask for a "dofollow" link.
